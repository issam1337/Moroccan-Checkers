SPANISH DRAUGHTS — KEY RULES

  • Board: 8×8, pieces on dark squares. Red (you) at bottom, White (AI) at top.
  • Men: Move and capture forward only (diagonally).
  • Kings (♛): Flying kings — move any number of squares diagonally in any direction.
  • Promotion: A man reaching the last row becomes a king. Sequence ends on promotion.
  • Mandatory capture: You must capture if possible.
  • Majority rule: You must take the maximum number of pieces.
  • Quality rule: If counts are equal, a king must capture instead of a man.
  • Multi-jump: After capturing, continue if more captures are available.
